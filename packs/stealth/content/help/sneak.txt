sneak
sneak on|off
Try to go unnoticed. While you're sneaking, each person who could see
you might miss you arriving or leaving, picking something up, handing
something over or drawing a weapon. They might see it as usual, or catch
you trying to avoid notice. You won't know who saw what.

Sneak stays on until you turn it off, even if you log out, and your
prompt shows [sneaking] while it's on.
