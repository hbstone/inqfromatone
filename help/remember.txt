keywords: remember, recognition, names, strangers, forget, rename
commands: remember, forget, rename
see also: shortdesc

Strangers are known by how they look ("a bearded man"). Once you learn
someone's name in play, you can remember them by it.

remember                     who you remember
remember <someone> <name>    remember someone here by a name
remember <someone>           what you remember about them
remember <someone> <note>    add a private note about someone you know
rename <someone> <new name>  change the name you know them by
forget <someone> memories    clear your notes about them
forget <someone>             forget them entirely (notes first)

Once remembered, you see that name and can type it. Each name needs a
word of its own, so two people you know can't be called just "Tom".
