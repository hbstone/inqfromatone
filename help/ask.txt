keywords: ask, questions
commands: ask
see also: say, sayto

ask <someone> [about] <topic>
Ask someone about something, in front of the room. A player answers in
their own words. An NPC answers from what it knows: about itself, the
place, what it has seen (ask about the bearded man) or what it has been
told. Some keep what they know to themselves, unless they know and like
you.
