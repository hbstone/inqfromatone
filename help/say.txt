keywords: say, speech, talking
commands: say, sayto
see also: whisper, emote, ask, tell

say <message>
sayto <someone> <message>
Speak aloud. This command is in character. Everyone in the room hears
it. Say speaks to the room; sayto speaks to one person in front of
everyone:

  You say to a mustachioed bartender, "The stage is running late."

Some NPCs remember what's said straight to them, and may repeat it when
someone asks.
