keywords: equipment, wear, wield, remove
commands: wear, wield, remove
see also: score

wear <item>
wield <item>
remove <item>
Put on clothing or armor, take up a weapon, or take something off again.
Wear and wield work the same way; use whichever reads right. Each piece
goes in its own slot (a hat on your head, a weapon in hand), one item
per slot. Some equipment changes your stats while you have it on. Score
shows what you're wearing.
