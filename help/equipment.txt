keywords: equipment, wear, wield, remove
commands: wear, wield, remove
see also: score

wear <item>
wield <item>
remove <item>
Put on clothing or armor, brandish a weapon, or take something off.
Wear and wield are identical, use whichever you prefer. Each piece goes
in its own slot (a hat on your head, a weapon in hand), one item per slot.
Score shows what you're wearing.

Some equipment may change your stats while worn.
