aiusage
How much the AI that answers some NPC questions has been used: calls in
the last hour against the hourly limit, then calls, tokens and a rough
cost for today and this month, and how often an NPC fell back to its usual
answer today (cooldown, over budget, refused, errors).

The cost is an estimate from token counts and covers only this game. The
Anthropic Console has the real bill. Set MUD_AI_MONTHLY_BUDGET to your
monthly limit there (in dollars) to see a percentage.

Meant for staff.
