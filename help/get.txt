keywords: get, take, pick up
commands: get
see also: drop, put, items

get <item>
get <item> [from] <container>
Pick something up from the floor, or take it out of a container you're
carrying or one in the room. People in the current room see you do it.

Items can be picked out by number: get 2.key takes the second key, and
get 3*arrow takes three arrows from a stack. Part of a word will do:
get bri finds a brick.
